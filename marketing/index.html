Most parks duct-tape POS + booking + memberships + waivers + door access. Each system charges, each one doesn't know about the others, each cashier learns a different UX.

✗ Square for POS, Acuity for lessons

Owner questions

The actual blockers, answered.

What does migration actually look like?

About a week. We import customers, products (with photos), and recent sales history from Square via CSV or direct API. We help you reconcile membership balances, then dual-run for ~1 week so you can compare totals. Day-of switchover takes ~30 min before opening. Square stays as backup for 30 days.

What about my Square card reader?

You can keep using it — skateOS rings the sale, you swipe on Square. Two systems, one workflow. When you're ready, you can move card processing to Helcim (~$6k/yr savings at typical volume), but that's optional and post-launch.

What if you go out of business?

The source code lives on GitHub and you can fork it. You always get an export of all your data in standard CSV/JSON. There's no lock-in. We exist because we own a skatepark — we're not a VC-funded company that disappears.

Do we get support?

Email on Starter, chat + Slack on Pro, dedicated engineer on Enterprise. Most issues get a response within hours, not days. We dogfood the product — if skateOS breaks at 2nd Nature, we fix it that morning.

Can we customize it?

Yes — every setting that makes sense for a park is tunable. Tip presets, loyalty rates, party packages, tier thresholds, receipt footer, email templates, even the staff kiosk colors. Pro plans can request feature changes; Enterprise gets custom builds.

Will my staff need training?

POS is the same product-grid + cart pattern as Square. Most cashiers pick it up in 30 min. The kiosk for clock-in is 2 taps. We do a video walkthrough during onboarding + you can demo with the practice mode toggle (fake sales, no real charges).

What about payments + processing fees?

We're agnostic. Keep Square as your processor (no change), or switch to Helcim for interchange-plus pricing (~2.4% vs Square's 2.9% + 10¢). skateOS rings every sale either way — your processor is just where the card swipe lands.

Are you GDPR / PCI compliant?

We never see card numbers — those go directly from the reader to your processor. Customer data is encrypted at rest in Supabase (SOC2). Granular customer-data export + delete on request. We're a small team — ask us about anything specific your insurance carrier wants confirmed.
