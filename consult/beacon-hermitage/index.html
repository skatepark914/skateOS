The Inn

An old house, kept calm on purpose.

Beacon Hermitage sits in a 19th-century home on Route 9D, a few minutes from the river and the galleries. It's adults-only and deliberately small — three rooms, a shared lounge, a garden to sit in, and the option of an on-site massage when you really want to let go.

Mornings bring a continental and buffet breakfast with fresh fruit and juice. Park free at the door, settle in, and let the weekend get slow.

Straight talk on Booking.com & your fees

We won't oversell this, so here's the honest version:

  • The real saving is direct bookings. Every reservation through Booking.com costs you roughly 15% commission. Every guest who books on your site instead pays you direct — you keep that 15%. This site exists to win those direct bookings.
  • Calendar sync is real, but not magic. We link your Booking.com / Airbnb calendars by iCal so booked dates block across platforms. Honest caveat: iCal updates on a delay (not instant) and passes only dates, not rates — it cuts double-bookings way down, but isn't a 100% guarantee.
  • Want true real-time, two-way sync? Booking.com only allows that through an approved channel manager — a small monthly tool we'd set up for you, not a custom hookup. For three rooms we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth it.

Bottom line: nobody can lower Booking.com's commission. What we do is help you need it less — by sending guests to your own door.

The honest part on SEO

  • It's a slow burn, not a switch. SEO compounds over months — it won't flip you off Booking.com overnight.
  • We won't out-rank Booking.com for "hotels in Beacon." Their site is a giant. Where you win is your own name, Google Maps, and local searches — capturing people who were already going to find you, so they book direct instead of costing you 15%.
  • The goal isn't to ditch Booking.com — it's to need it less. Keep it for discovery; keep the repeat and direct guests for yourself.

The math — and the industry behind it

  • OTAs like Booking.com take 15–25% commission on every reservation — the 2026 industry standard.
  • Independent inns aim for 30–50% of their bookings direct — and every direct booking skips that OTA cut entirely.
  • Run it on a ~$500 two-night stay: that's ~$75–125 gone to commission on Booking.com — but yours on a direct booking. Shift even ~3 stays a month to direct and you keep roughly $2,700–4,500 a year.
  • So the $1,199 build pays for itself in a few months, and Essential Care ($49/mo) covers itself with about one direct booking a month.

Conservative estimates — your real numbers depend on your rates and volume. The point: the math tips in your favor fast.
