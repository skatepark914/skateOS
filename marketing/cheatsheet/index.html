🛹 Front Desk Cheat Sheet

2nd Nature Park · skateOS · app.skateos.com
v1 · launch
⭐ The one rule: ring EVERY sale in skateOS. Cash, card, comp — all of it. That's how the numbers stay right.

💳 Make a sale — 4 taps

  1. Tap the items (or scan a barcode / member card).
  2. Pick the customer — search their name, or leave Walk-in.
  3. Take payment (see below).
  4. Tap the big Charge button. Done — offer receipt.
CashTake cash. Screen shows change due.
CardSwipe on the Square reader, then pick "Credit Card" → Charge.
CompUse the Comp button + reason.

🪪 Check a skater in

  1. Check-In page → type their name (or scan member card / QR).
  2. Tap their name → Check In. Watch for the green WELCOME.
  3. Punch card auto-decrements; low-punch warning pops if they're running out.
No waiver? → resolve first.
  • Email/text them the waiver link, or have them sign on the iPad — then check in.

🎓 Lessons & parties

  • Lessons page → tap a slot → pick skater + instructor → Save.
  • Party / birthday → Tools → Party quote.
  • Day-of: check the "Coming up" strip on Check-In.

🌙 End of day

  1. Sales → Close out day → count the drawer (use the denomination helper).
  2. Close park on Check-In → checks everyone out.
  3. Lock up, helmets/loaner gear away.

⌨️ Speed tips

  • / jump to product search · 1–6 quick-add top sellers
  • ⌘+Enter complete sale · ? all shortcuts
  • Mid-sale, someone walks up? Hold sale → ring them → Restore.

🆘 If something's off

  • Wrong sale? Void it (within 10 min) — for returns use Refund (reason required).
  • Card reader down? Take cash or text the customer a pay link.
  • App acting weird? Tap the version chip (top-right) to hard-refresh.
  • Stuck → text Doug.
