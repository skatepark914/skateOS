skateOS · payments plan

Use Square through skateOS now → Stripe Tap to Pay next

You don't have to wait on anything to launch. skateOS becomes your full register today, riding on the Square account + reader you already have. Then we move the card processing to Stripe Tap to Pay (tap on the iPhone) when it's ready.

The one thing to know about Square hardware

Square's reader/terminal only processes through Square — it can't be wired into skateOS to run a different processor. So "using Square through skateOS" means: skateOS is the register; Square processes the card as a swipe alongside it. skateOS owns the store, inventory, and reports; Square just moves the plastic. That's the bridge — and it works with the hardware you have right now.

Now — the Square bridge (launch with this)

Live today · no new hardware, no waiting

skateOS rings · Square swipes

1. Ring the sale in skateOS→ 2. Card? swipe on the Square reader→ 3. Mark "Card" + Complete in skateOS
  • Cash → take cash, skateOS shows change due. Done.
  • Card → swipe on your existing Square reader (Square processes it, your rates/account unchanged), then hit Complete in skateOS marked Card.
  • skateOS is the source of truth for the store — inventory, receipts, customers, reports. Square is just the card rail.
✅ Why launch on this: zero new purchase, zero waiting on the Stripe account, and you start building trust in the real app immediately. The only cost is a quick second step at the reader for card sales (goes away at the next phase).
