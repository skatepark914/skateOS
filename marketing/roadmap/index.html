skateOS × AI · internal plan

From "new system" to fully trusted & live — in 6 months

How we use AI incrementally to run 2nd Nature: start where it literally can't break anything, prove the numbers against Square, and expand only as trust is earned — until skateOS is the operating layer of the park.

The principle: trust is earned, not assumed. Every phase below starts low-stakes (parallel tools, drafts a human approves, shadow-mode against Square) and only moves to higher-stakes once the prior step has proven accurate in the real world. AI never sends money, emails, or orders on its own until that specific job has earned it.

The 6-month arc

Month1
Parallel tools — zero risk in motion
AI builds internal tools that can't touch live operations
No risk
  • Mixture apparel order builder — the flagship example: a shared, multi-person order tool (Lucas, Jon, Doug each submit, AI averages into a final order, with live mockups + real supplier colors). Built by AI, lives beside operations, touches no customer or money. → see it
  • Shadow-run vs Square — skateOS runs alongside Square; AI reconciles totals daily and flags any gap (it already caught 488 un-imported Square orders).
  • AI builds the internal status board, migrations, and reconciliation tooling.
Trust milestone: the team uses an AI-built tool daily, and skateOS's numbers match Square to the dollar.
Month2
Go live at the front desk
AI assists; a human still makes every call
Low risk
  • Go live on the Square bridge — skateOS rings every sale, your existing Square reader swipes the cards. No new hardware, nothing blocked. (payments plan →)
  • Full store, stocked incrementally — start with 2nd Nature decks, add a category at a time as you trust it. (Deck Pilot is live in the real POS now.)
  • AI handles the safe, repetitive layer: receipts, daily close-out math, low-stock alerts, the morning digest email.
  • Endgame: swap card processing to Stripe Tap to Pay on iPhone — tap the card right in skateOS, drop the extra swipe + the Square fees.
Trust milestone: a full week of skateOS sales reconcile clean against Square, and staff trust the flow.
Month3
Insights & gentle automation
AI surfaces what matters; humans act on it
Low risk
  • Weekly/monthly performance reports + partner P&L to Jon, written automatically.
  • At-risk-customer and re-order lists; AI suggests, owner decides.
  • Opt-in reminders (lessons, membership renewals) and AI-drafted marketing campaigns — owner sends.
Trust milestone: a decision the owner made off an AI insight pays off (recovered member, avoided stockout).
Month4
Customer-facing — supervised
AI touches customers, with a human in the loop
Medium risk
  • Online ordering / pre-orders (the same shared-form pattern as Mixture, now customer-facing) and the self-serve member portal.
  • Digital waivers fully automated (Smartwaiver → auto-stamped on the customer).
  • Inbound form submissions → AI drafts the response, owner approves.
  • Brivo door → auto check-in, once it's proven on a few real taps.
Trust milestone: customers self-serve (orders, waivers, check-ins) with no front-desk error.
Month5
Scale the pattern
One park proven → repeatable
Medium risk
  • The "many people feed one shared database" pattern (proven on the Mixture form) generalizes to multi-park / white-label onboarding.
  • AI watches for anomalies: refund spikes, capacity overages, reconciliation drift — and alerts before they become problems.
  • AI-built onboarding so a second park can come online in days, not months.
Trust milestone: the system runs a full month with AI catching issues before a human noticed them.
Month6
Trusted autonomy
AI runs the routine; humans set direction
Earned
  • The jobs that earned trust now run on their own with oversight: auto-reconcile, auto-reorder, reminder + marketing cadence, partner reporting.
  • Square is cancelled — skateOS is the system of record.
  • Owner's time shifts from running the software to running the business; AI handles the operating layer.
Trust milestone: skateOS is fully live, Square is gone, and the park runs on an AI-operated stack you trust.
