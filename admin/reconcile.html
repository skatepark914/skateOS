?How variance is calculated

For each day:

  • · skateOS gross = sum of sales.total for the day, net of refunds, excluding voided.
  • · Square gross = what you type. Should match Square's Dashboard daily summary.
  • · Variance = Square − skateOS. Positive means Square rang up more.
Likely causes of variance > $5:
  • → A sale rang in Square but not skateOS (cashier defaulted to old habit). Find it in Square + retroactively log in skateOS.
  • → Refund processed in skateOS but not Square (or vice-versa). Sync them.
  • → Tax / tip configuration differs between systems. Check Settings.
  • → Cash sale rung without entry in one of the two systems.
