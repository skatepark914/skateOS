Straight talk on what this actually does

We won't oversell it, so here's the honest version of where the value is:

  • You're losing leads to phone tag. 67–70% of people would rather book online than call, and 40% of bookings happen after hours — every missed call or slow DM reply is a client who books someone else.
  • No-shows cost you real money. Automatic text/email reminders cut no-shows by 38–50% — on a $400 day-train or a $3,000 Board & Train, that adds up fast.
  • It won't replace your phone — and shouldn't. Some clients will always call, and that's good. This captures the ones who'd never call, and kills the back-and-forth on the rest. You're not paying any "commission" to anyone — there's no OTA in dog training. The value is your time, more booked evals, and fewer empty slots.

The honest part on SEO

  • It's a slow burn, not a switch. SEO compounds over months — it won't fill your calendar overnight.
  • Where you win is local + reviews. You won't out-rank the big directories on generic terms, but "dog trainer Stamford / Greenwich / Darien," Google Maps, and a strong review profile are very winnable — and that's where serious local clients look.
  • Reviews do the heavy lifting. For a trainer, a steady stream of 5-star Google reviews beats almost any other marketing. We make leaving one effortless.
